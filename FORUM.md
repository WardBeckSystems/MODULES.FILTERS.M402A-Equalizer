## M402As' compared to M460LAs' EQ

_WBS INFO · Jul 25, 2012|45_

> Hi to y'all!! 
>
> I know these are different beasts from different Era but i was wondering what was the "tone" of the 402As EQs compared to the M460LAs.. i have a pair of 402As that aren't racked yet and i just wanted to know.
>
> Thanks
>
> Phil

— **pawajari**, Jul 25, 2012|45

> yes different beasts. 
>
> they are passive filters closer to an API. (a series of R-C networks switched into play) some argue smoother more consistent. this is because of the simplicity of the circuit. It's traditional, very expensive to hand wire, and a very space consuming design) the early FET opamps have a slow slew rate. Transformer on the input of the EQ... many good things going on inside. I think the 402B had LM301s in them... 
>
> The 460 is an active inductor filter. (a series of Op amps acting as variable inductors) some say they introduce phase problems because of their complexity. It's cheaper, it doesn't go CLICK CLICK CLICK on air as you make changes... quick to use. very clean. 
>
> are the 402A's edge connectors? or the stupid amphenol top thing?

— **pawajari**, Jul 25, 2012|45

> The connector was a mil-spec 14 pin connector not by Amphenol but by a Winchester. Card edged connectors were on the revision A of this module series. The transformer is of course a custom designed unit by Hammond, and is an excellent microphone preamplifyer unit!! Of course if the switches are clean there sill not be a click, click, click. You will hear or more aptly "feel" a difference when you switch between the frequencies. 
>
> Originally the straights did not have an LM201, that came withe the A and B. The LM201 of course evolved to the LM301. At the time in the late 1960's and early 1970's Dad did a lot of design work with/for National Semiconductor - thus the Fairchild components were phased out, and dad's National components came in to more common use.
>
> The Winchester connector is still available. With 14 pins, connector block and hood you are looking at $100, as I just bought one for a racking project.

— **pawajari**, Jul 26, 2012|45

> YEs that is exactly what you use. A normal EDAC from one of your spare M460 module spaces, you can use 1 side, install the pad as per Tony's dwg's, and carry on!!!
>
> OR you could make the investment and procure (2) card edge connectors that are single sided. But they are no-longer commonplace and a pain in the ass to obtain. We used EDAC of course (local manufacturer) but other manufacturers work as well. Just be certain to use a connector with no less than 10 microns of gold on the contacts. You can and will experience migration of 1 material to the other (PCB gold contact to an un-plated EDAC wiper) over time and this will cause intermittencies. I advise a standard plating of greater than 20 microns. Some alternate manufacturers offer various plating options for cost savings, but EDAC's standard is of course perfect!!
